# Literature and coverage

The October 2026 Open Problems Catalog records the exact thirteen-gon, its negative linear coefficient, positivity through size twelve and the uniqueness of the negative coefficient at size thirteen. Mantra Patel's paper treats the same instance and finite minimality using trace formulas and exact certificates. Bowen Zuo's paper cites both. The all-size sign rule and asymptotic results are the subject of this release. [Catalog proof](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/solutions/seven-more-2026-10-09/discrete/Q3499-proof.md) · [Patel's paper](https://github.com/mantrakp04/research-papers/blob/c0505c4261045f1ab5760a024f1dfc4fb8f725a8/arxiv/polygon_ehrhart/main.tex).

The original positivity question concerns ordinary monomial coefficients for the full polygon face lattice. Results for the circular fence, order-polynomial variables shifted by one, and real zeros of the Ehrhart numerator use different invariants. The paper gives the endpoint convolution and credits the crown trace methods it uses. [Lundström–Saud Maia Leite](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2025.104304) · [Huang, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22767v2) · [Cao–Fu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40496v2).

On 26 November 2026 the research team checked arXiv, journal records and Crossref, and searched GitHub repository metadata and source text, including the exact rational numerator and denominator. The earlier computations above matched. The sources inspected contained no all-size classification equivalent to the one stated in the paper. Google Scholar presented a verification page and supplied no readable native list. This is a dated account of the search.
